Cardigan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-15

Cardigan is a card-game score keeper that runs entirely on your device.

Data not collected

Cardigan does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data collection of any kind.

Everything you enter — player names, scores, game history — is stored in a local database on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere. The app works fully offline and makes no network connections.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
