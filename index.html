Cardigan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-04

Cardigan is a score pad for card games. Your games stay on your phone. The app sends a small amount of anonymous usage data, and you can turn it off.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (player names, scores, game history) is saved on your phone and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app sends

Cardigan sends a few anonymous events so we can see how the app is used: tapping New game, adding players, starting a game, saving a round, and finishing a game. With those it sends how many players were in the game, how many rounds it lasted, which house rules were on, whether the game ended by reaching the target score or was ended early, whether it was your first game, the target score you chose, and which round each saved round was, plus the app version, your phone model and iOS version, and your device's language and time zone.

It does not send player names, anything you type, or your scores.

There's no account and no sign-in, so none of this is linked to you. It's labelled with a random ID that isn't connected to your name, your email, your Apple ID, or any advertising ID. Cardigan doesn't use the advertising identifier and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. IP addresses are thrown away, not stored.

The data is handled by PostHog, on servers in the European Union.

Turning it off

Open the rules page from the home screen, go to Configure, and switch off "Share anonymous usage data". Nothing more is sent.

No ads

Cardigan has no ads, and no advertising or marketing trackers. It never has and it never will.
