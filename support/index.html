Cardigan — Support

Cardigan is a score pad for Hearts. Your games are saved on your phone.

Getting help

Email: cardigan.scoring@gmail.com

Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where are my games stored?
On your phone, and only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud backup, so deleting the app deletes your game history.

Can I change the rules?
Yes. Open the rules page from the home screen to set the target score and turn the jack of diamonds rule on or off. Each game keeps the rules it started with, so changing them won't affect a game already in progress.

I made a mistake entering a round.
Use undo on the game screen to remove the most recent round.

Why won't it accept my round?
Hearts must add up to 13 across all players, and only one player can take the queen of spades. If the numbers don't work, Cardigan asks you to correct them rather than saving a hand that couldn't have happened.
